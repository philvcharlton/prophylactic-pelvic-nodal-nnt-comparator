Uncertainty policy. The displayed trial NNTs are point estimates calculated from published arm-level percentages at a common time point. They are not confidence intervals for NNT. NNT confidence intervals are only valid when based on an uncertainty estimate for the absolute risk difference; intervals that cross zero imply a discontinuous range spanning benefit, infinity, and harm. The reusable calculation module supports risk-difference confidence intervals when adequate inputs are available. See the uncertainty documentation.

Method

How the calculation works

For an outcome where a higher percentage is desirable, the absolute benefit is treatment rate minus comparator rate. For an adverse event, the direction is reversed. The exact reciprocal is shown to two decimal places.

NNT = 1 ÷ absolute risk reduction
