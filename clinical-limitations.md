# Clinical limitations

- Results apply to trial populations and treatment protocols, not automatically to every contemporary patient.
- Time-specific NNTs may change with follow-up duration.
- Competing risks and subsequent therapies are not modelled.
- Point-estimate NNTs do not communicate uncertainty by themselves.
- Outcomes overlap and must not be summed.
- Absence of a statistically significant difference does not establish equivalence.
- The custom calculator checks arithmetic, not clinical comparability of the entered rates.
- The tool does not generate a treatment recommendation.
